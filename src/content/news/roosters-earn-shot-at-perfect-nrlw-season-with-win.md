The Sydney Roosters have secured their place in the NRLW grand final, maintaining an unblemished record following a disciplined 20-10 victory over the Canberra Raiders. The win preserves the Tricolours' prospects of a perfect season, setting the stage for a high-stakes premiership decider that could see the side enter the history books.

Throughout the semi-final clash, the Roosters demonstrated the clinical efficiency that has defined their 2024 campaign, neutralizing Canberra’s attacking threats with a robust defensive line and superior tactical kicking. While the Raiders showed glimpses of resilience in the second half, the Sydney side’s ability to control the ruck and capitalise on field position ultimately proved the difference in a high-pressure environment.

> "The pursuit of a perfect season is no longer just a statistical anomaly; it is a testament to a squad that has mastered the art of relentless consistency and mental fortitude under immense pressure."

As the focus shifts to the grand final, the Roosters face the weight of history in their quest for an undefeated premiership. For Canberra, the result marks the conclusion of a valiant season, while the league prepares for a finale that promises to showcase the absolute peak of the women's professional game in Australia.
