Melbourne is facing an unprecedented surge in targeted burglaries and retail thefts as the soaring value of rare Pokémon trading cards transforms a nostalgic hobby into a high-stakes target for organised crime. Local law enforcement agencies have reported a significant uptick in sophisticated heists across the metropolitan area, with specialised taskforces now monitoring secondary marketplaces where these high-value assets are frequently liquidated.

The driving force behind this criminal trend is the astronomical appreciation of vintage collectibles, exemplified by the record-breaking sale of a single Pikachu card for over $22 million earlier this year. As these cardboard assets become comparable in value to fine art or bullion, small businesses and private collectors in Victoria have found themselves increasingly vulnerable to coordinated robberies aimed at high-yield inventory.

> "What was once a schoolyard pastime has morphed into a volatile shadow economy, where the allure of quick, untraceable profit is driving a dangerous new wave of local property crime."

Victoria Police are urging the community to exercise heightened caution when conducting high-value transactions and to secure physical collections with professional-grade security measures. As this 'Poké-crime' wave shows no signs of abating, the distinction between a childhood game and a serious financial liability has never been more pronounced for Melburnians.
