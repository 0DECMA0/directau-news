Indonesian search and rescue authorities have confirmed that five individuals have perished and 41 remain unaccounted for after a passenger ferry carrying 271 people caught fire in open waters. The vessel was transiting through the archipelago when the blaze broke out, forcing many to abandon ship into the surrounding seas in a desperate bid for survival.

Emergency responders and local vessels have been deployed to the site to assist in the recovery and search efforts. While over 200 passengers have been successfully pulled from the water, the fate of those still missing remains the primary concern for regional maritime coordination centres as the operation continues under challenging conditions.

> "This tragedy serves as a harrowing reminder of the inherent risks in regional maritime transit and the urgent need for stringent safety protocols across our northern neighbours’ waterways."

Investigations into the origin of the fire are expected to commence once the search operation concludes. For now, the focus remains on locating survivors in a race against time, as families await word on their loved ones amidst one of the region's most significant maritime incidents this year.
