Two Australian nationals have survived a harrowing helicopter crash during aerial firefighting operations in Indonesia’s South Kalimantan province. Local authorities confirmed the aircraft lost control and plunged into a river while conducting water-bombing missions, resulting in the tragic loss of an Indonesian crew member at the scene.

Footage recovered from the site shows the aircraft retrieving water moments before it appeared to suffer a critical mechanical failure. While the Australians were successfully pulled from the wreckage and are currently receiving medical care, the incident serves as a stark reminder of the immense risks faced by international aviation crews operating in volatile environmental conditions.

> "The intersection of humanitarian effort and extreme environmental risk remains a volatile frontier for the pilots who safeguard our region's ecology."

Indonesian aviation safety officials have launched a full investigation into the cause of the disaster. The Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade has been notified and is expected to provide ongoing consular support to the survivors and their families during the recovery process.
