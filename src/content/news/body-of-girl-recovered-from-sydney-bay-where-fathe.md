New South Wales Police divers have recovered the body of a young girl from the waters of Blackwattle Bay in Sydney’s inner west this afternoon, following the earlier discovery of a man’s remains in the same vicinity.

Emergency services were called to the scene early Monday morning after a passerby raised the alarm. Authorities have since confirmed the deceased man was the child’s father, leading detectives to treat the incident as a deeply distressing domestic occurrence that has left the local Glebe community in shock.

> "In the wake of such profound loss, we are forced to confront the silent fractures in our social fabric that often go unnoticed until it is tragically too late."

A significant police presence remains at the bay as forensic specialists conduct a thorough examination of the area. A report is being prepared for the Coroner, and officials have requested that any witnesses or individuals with dashcam footage from the Glebe Foreshore contact Crime Stoppers to assist with the ongoing investigation.
