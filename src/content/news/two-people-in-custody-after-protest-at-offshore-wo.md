Two individuals have been taken into police custody following a high-stakes protest at a Woodside Energy offshore platform, situated approximately 200 kilometres off the coast of Western Australia. The incident, which unfolded in the early hours, involved the unauthorised occupation of the facility as a demonstration against the company’s ongoing natural gas operations in the region.

Western Australian Police confirmed the arrests on Tuesday, stating that the protesters were removed from the structure without further incident. The offshore facility, a critical component of Australia's energy infrastructure, was temporarily placed under security protocols while authorities coordinated the response from the mainland to ensure the safety of all personnel on board.

> "This incident underscores the escalating friction between industrial energy security and increasingly assertive climate activism within the nation's resource heartland."

Woodside Energy has reaffirmed its commitment to the safety of its personnel and the integrity of its assets, noting that while the right to peaceful protest is respected, the occupation of offshore platforms poses significant safety risks. Legal proceedings are expected to follow as the industry grapples with the logistical challenges of securing remote maritime installations against targeted civil disobedience.
