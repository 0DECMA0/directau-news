The Iranian high command has issued a directive to its military forces to meet any potential foreign aggression with immediate and unrestrained force, according to senior intelligence reports. This mandate signals a departure from previous tactical restraint, indicating that Tehran is prepared to escalate current hostilities into a broader regional conflict should the United States or Israel initiate further strikes against Iranian interests.

International observers suggest this shift in posture places the Middle East on the precipice of a significant security realignment. As diplomatic channels remain strained, the prospect of an expanded theatre of operations now poses a direct challenge to global maritime stability and energy security, prompting urgent reviews of strategic contingencies across Western capitals, including Canberra.

> "The transition from strategic patience to a doctrine of unrestrained retaliation represents a critical threshold that threatens to transform contained skirmishes into a transcontinental security crisis."

As the situation develops, the Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade continues to monitor the heightened risk environment, advising a policy of extreme caution. For now, the global community remains focused on whether back-channel communications can de-escalate a situation that many fear is rapidly moving beyond the point of conventional diplomacy.
