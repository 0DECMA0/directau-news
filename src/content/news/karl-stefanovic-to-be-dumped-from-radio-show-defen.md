Nine Network stalwart Karl Stefanovic is reportedly set to be removed from his upcoming radio program following the intense fallout from a controversial interview with British figure Tommy Robinson. The ABC understands that the decision was finalised after significant internal and public pushback regarding the editorial direction of the new venture.

The veteran broadcaster has spent the last 48 hours defending the segment, asserting that the conversation was a legitimate piece of journalism aimed at challenging a divisive individual. However, industry insiders suggest the move highlights a growing sensitivity among commercial networks toward content that risks alienating broad audiences or breaching community standards.

> "The intersection of commercial viability and editorial provocation is increasingly narrow, leaving little room for error when a platform is extended to polarising global figures."

As the dust settles, Stefanovic remains a cornerstone of the network's television line-up, yet his foray into this specific radio project appears to have reached a premature conclusion. The decision serves as a stark reminder of the rigorous scrutiny applied to modern Australian broadcasting in an era of heightened social accountability.
