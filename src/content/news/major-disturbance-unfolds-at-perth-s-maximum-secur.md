A serious security incident at a Perth maximum-security correctional facility has left a prison officer injured and several sections of the prison with extensive structural damage. Authorities confirmed that the disturbance broke out early this afternoon, necessitating a rapid response from the Special Operations Group to restore order within the high-security perimeter.

The Department of Justice has transitioned the facility into a total lockdown as emergency services treat the wounded officer and assess the scale of the destruction. Initial reports suggest that the unrest was concentrated in a specific wing, resulting in significant damage to infrastructure and internal fixtures before specialist units could successfully intervene.

> "This incident serves as a stark reminder of the inherent volatility within our maximum-security systems and the precarious nature of maintaining order in high-pressure environments."

Union representatives for the custodial staff have already signaled their intent to call for a comprehensive review of safety protocols following the altercation. A formal investigation into the catalyst of the disturbance is expected to commence once the facility is deemed fully secured and the extent of the repairs required is quantified.
