Member of Parliament Cory Bernardi has drawn sharp condemnation following the use of derogatory language directed at demonstrators outside South Australia’s Parliament House. Organisers of an anti-One Nation rally have formally denounced the MP's conduct, after he was heard labelling participants as 'incels' during a heated confrontation on the steps of the legislature.

The incident occurred amidst a volatile atmosphere as protesters gathered to voice their opposition to the party's platform. Witnesses and rally coordinators argue that such remarks from an elected official not only inflame public tension but also fall well below the standard of decorum expected of a representative of the Crown in a modern democracy.

> "When the language of the chamber descends into the vernacular of online fringe groups, the dignity of the democratic process is inevitably the first casualty."

While Bernardi has long been a polarising figure in the Australian political landscape, this latest exchange has prompted renewed calls for a code of conduct review. As of Wednesday evening, the MP’s office has yet to provide a formal response to the allegations of verbal misconduct or the subsequent backlash from community advocacy groups.
