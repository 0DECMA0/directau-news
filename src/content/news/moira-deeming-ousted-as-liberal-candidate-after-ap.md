The Victorian Liberal Party’s state executive has formally revoked Moira Deeming’s candidacy for the upcoming November election, effectively terminating her career as a party representative. The decision marks the final chapter in a protracted stand-off involving a requested apology, reflecting a firm stance by the leadership to enforce internal discipline ahead of the polls.

The executive's move follows months of internal tension surrounding Deeming’s participation in a controversial public demonstration. Despite several opportunities to reconcile with the party’s front bench through a formal apology, the impasse remained unresolved, leaving the executive committee with little choice but to move for her dismissal to protect the party's electoral viability.

> "In the high-stakes theatre of state politics, the sacrifice of an individual candidacy is often the price paid for the perceived restoration of party integrity."

With her candidacy now officially rescinded, the Liberal Party must pivot quickly to find a replacement in what is expected to be a tightly contested election. While Deeming’s future in the legislative assembly remains certain for now, her influence within the Liberal fold has been completely extinguished, signaling a significant shift in the Victorian political landscape.
