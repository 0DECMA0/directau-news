The Sydney Roosters have secured a historic NRL premiership, defeating the Newcastle Knights 19-18 in a heart-stopping Grand Final. A clinical Daly Cherry-Evans field goal in the final minutes proved the difference, capping off a relentless encounter between two of the competition's elite heavyweights.

In a match defined by bruising defensive sets and tactical precision, neither side was able to break clear for the duration of the eighty minutes. The Knights showed remarkable grit, fighting back from an early deficit to trade blows with the Bondi-based outfit, yet ultimately fell short by the narrowest of margins in what will be remembered as a modern classic.

This victory cements the Roosters' standing as a premier force in the modern era, while for Newcastle, the result marks a valiant but agonizing end to a standout season.
