Shaun Mannagh has delivered a career-defining performance, steering Geelong to a commanding 41-point victory over the Brisbane Lions on their home turf. The mature-age recruit finished with an extraordinary tally of five goals and 30 disposals, a feat that silenced the Gabba faithful and cemented the Cats' status as genuine premiership heavyweights.

The Lions, usually formidable within their Queensland fortress, were systematically dismantled by Chris Scott’s tactical precision and clinical finishing. Despite flashes of resistance from Brisbane’s engine room, Geelong maintained a relentless defensive pressure, effectively neutralising the Lions' transition play and capitalising on turnovers with surgical efficiency.

> "Geelong’s systematic dissection of the Lions suggests a shift in the AFL power dynamic, proving that veteran tactical discipline remains the ultimate counter to home-ground advantage."

This statement win significantly bolsters the Cats' standing in the top eight as the finals series looms, while leaving Brisbane with pressing questions regarding their defensive structural integrity. Mannagh’s breakout night not only secures the crucial four points but highlights the enduring depth of a Geelong list that continues to defy external expectations of a rebuild.
