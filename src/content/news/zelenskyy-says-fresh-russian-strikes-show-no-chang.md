Overnight, Kyiv endured a heavy barrage of ballistic missiles, while Ukraine launched a significant retaliatory drone offensive targeting the Russian capital in a bid to disrupt military infrastructure.

The exchange occurs as Washington intensifies efforts to broker a ceasefire, a move that currently appears to have hit a stalemate. The scale of the latest aerial campaign suggests that military strategy continues to take precedence over negotiation tables, leaving regional stability in a precarious state as both sides seek to maximise their leverage.

As both nations bolster their long-range strike capabilities, the prospects for a near-term resolution remain dim.
