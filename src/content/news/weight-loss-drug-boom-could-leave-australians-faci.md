Healthcare experts are sounding the alarm over a potential surge in bone fractures and debilitating falls as Australians flock to the latest generation of weight-loss medications. Exercise & Sports Science Australia (ESSA) warns that the rapid reduction in body mass often masks a dangerous decline in skeletal muscle, which is vital for physical stability.

This pharmaceutical trend, while effective for metabolic management, is creating a silent deficit in physical strength across the nation. Without professional guidance and dedicated resistance training, patients risk trading one health issue for another, potentially leading to a significant loss of independence and a heightened burden on the national aged care sector.

> "In the rush for a clinical solution to obesity, we must ensure we are not inadvertently engineering a future of fragility for the Australian public."

Medical practitioners are now being urged to recommend holistic care models that prioritise muscle preservation. Ensuring that weight loss is sustainable and safe requires a shift in focus from the scales to functional strength, ensuring that the quest for a leaner physique does not result in a more vulnerable population.
