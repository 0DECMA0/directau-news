A Supreme Court jury has delivered a guilty verdict against North Queensland grazier Darryl Valroy Young, following the fatal shooting of three neighbours at a property gate in Bogie in 2022. The decision, reached after intensive deliberations, concludes a trial that has gripped the state and highlighted the devastating consequences of escalating rural disputes in remote regions.

The victims—Mervyn and Maree Schwarz, along with Maree’s son Robert Schwarz—were fatally wounded during a confrontation regarding a boundary fence. Throughout the proceedings, the court heard harrowing testimony regarding the long-standing tensions that preceded the violence, painting a somber picture of a community fractured by a sudden and irreversible act of aggression.

> "This verdict serves as a grim reminder that when the rule of law is abandoned in favour of a firearm, the cost to our social fabric is both absolute and permanent."

Young now faces a mandatory sentence of life imprisonment, the standard penalty for such crimes under Queensland law. As the Bogie district begins the long process of healing, the legal system has provided a definitive answer to a tragedy that remains one of the most significant and distressing criminal cases in recent regional history.
