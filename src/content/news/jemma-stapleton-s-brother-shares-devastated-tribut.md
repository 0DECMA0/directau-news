The Australian sporting community is mourning the sudden passing of Jemma Stapleton, a 25-year-old sprinter and Stawell Gift finalist, following her tragic departure in Thailand. News of the athlete’s passing has sent shockwaves through the Victorian athletics circuit, where she was regarded as both a formidable competitor and a cherished figure among her peers.

Her brother, Sam Stapleton, led the tributes today, remembering his sister for her "bright smile" and the indomitable spirit she brought to the track. As the family navigates this profound loss while abroad, they have expressed deep gratitude for the overwhelming outpouring of grief and support flowing in from across the nation.

> "The premature departure of a gifted athlete in their prime leaves a void that transcends the sporting arena, echoing the shared vulnerability of our youth when far from home."

Details regarding the circumstances of her passing remain limited as the family works alongside consular officials to bring her home. The Stapleton family has requested privacy during this devastating period, while the athletics community prepares to honour the legacy of a young woman who left an indelible mark on Australian sprinting.
