Western Australian community leaders and advocates have been formally recognised at the 2027 Australian of the Year Awards in Perth, with the state's top honour awarded to a mother dedicated to vision impairment advocacy. Following her son’s permanent loss of sight, the recipient has spent years transforming a private family struggle into a robust support network that has significantly altered the landscape for disability services across the state.

The ceremony, held at Government House, underscored a recurring theme among this year's cohort: the power of lived experience in driving social reform. These awards serve as a testament to the resilience of the Western Australian spirit, highlighting individuals who have faced profound personal adversity and emerged as pillars of strength for the broader community.

> "The measure of a society is found in how its citizens transmute individual hardship into a collective legacy of progress and empathy."

As the winners prepare to represent Western Australia at the national awards in Canberra this coming January, their contributions remain a vital reminder of the impact one person can have on the public interest. The recognition of such deeply personal missions ensures that the voices of the vulnerable continue to be heard at the highest levels of our national conversation.
