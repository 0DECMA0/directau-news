Prime Minister Anthony Albanese has formally retracted his previous assertions regarding a $6 million federal grant awarded to the Marrickville Golf Club, admitting the funds were not designated for sewage infrastructure. The correction follows a significant discrepancy highlighted by Department of Infrastructure officials, who confirmed that the substantial allocation for the club in the Prime Minister’s own electorate was intended for other purposes.

The admission comes after weeks of parliamentary scrutiny concerning the transparency of local government grants. While the Prime Minister had originally defended the expenditure as a necessary fix for long-standing wastewater issues impacting the Inner West site, departmental testimony during recent hearings clarified that such works were never part of the approved funding scope.

> "The integrity of ministerial accountability rests on the precision of public statements; when department records diverge from executive claims, the resulting friction necessitates more than just a simple correction."

Opposition members have seized on the development, questioning the rigour of the vetting process for projects within the Prime Minister's seat of Grayndler. Despite the factual backtrack, Mr Albanese maintained that the investment would provide significant community benefits, though he acknowledged the error in his initial public characterisation of the project's technical requirements.
