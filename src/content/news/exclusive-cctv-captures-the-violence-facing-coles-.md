Exclusive CCTV footage obtained by 7.30 has laid bare a disturbing surge in physical aggression against retail employees, highlighting a series of high-stakes incidents within Coles supermarkets across the country. The vision depicts several alarming encounters, most notably an individual brandishing a hammer and a security officer being violently propelled into a self-checkout unit during a confrontation.

This evidence surfaces amidst a broader national conversation regarding the safety of essential workers in the retail sector. As social tensions and cost-of-living pressures continue to simmer, industry stakeholders report that verbal abuse has increasingly evolved into physical altercations, leaving staff members traumatised and the industry grappling for more effective deterrents to ensure workplace safety.

> "The erosion of civility in our retail hubs has shifted from occasional friction to a systemic threat, forcing frontline staff to navigate an environment of physical peril on a daily basis."

In response to the growing crisis, major retailers are investing heavily in body-worn cameras and advanced surveillance technology to protect their workforce. However, unions and industry leaders argue that technology alone cannot solve the problem, calling for more stringent legal penalties for offenders and a fundamental shift in societal respect for those providing essential services.
