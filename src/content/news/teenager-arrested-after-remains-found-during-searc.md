Victoria Police have arrested a teenager following the grim discovery of human remains during an extensive search for a missing 13-year-old girl in the state's west. Homicide Squad detectives were deployed to the regional site late yesterday, cordoning off the area as forensic experts commenced a detailed examination of the scene.

The suspect was taken into custody shortly after the find and is currently assisting investigators with their inquiries. While formal identification has not been finalised, the development has brought a somber conclusion to the multi-agency search operation that had mobilised local residents and emergency services over the past 24 hours.

> "The intersection of youthful vulnerability and such a catastrophic outcome leaves a permanent scar on the collective conscience of regional Victoria."

Authorities have established a secondary crime scene and are appealing to the public for any dashcam footage or information regarding suspicious activity in the area. As the investigation remains in its early stages, police have reaffirmed their commitment to providing answers for the grieving family while maintaining the integrity of the judicial process.
