A northern Victorian man has been acquitted of dangerous driving causing death following a high-profile trial in the County Court. On Wednesday, a jury returned a verdict of not guilty, concluding a rigorous legal proceeding regarding an incident where a woman lost her life after being struck by a trailer in Yarrawonga.

The court heard that the accused was transporting a vehicle on a trailer through the regional township when the fatal collision occurred. Throughout the trial, the defence focused on the technicalities of the impact and argued that the driver’s conduct did not reach the criminal threshold required for a conviction of dangerous driving under Victorian law.

> "This verdict underscores the immense burden of proof required when the justice system must weigh a tragic loss of life against the complexities of road safety statutes."

Following the delivery of the verdict, the man was discharged and permitted to leave the court. While the prosecution had alleged that the accused failed to maintain a safe standard of operation, the jury’s decision marks the final legal resolution for a case that has deeply resonated within the Yarrawonga community.
