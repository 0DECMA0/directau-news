French authorities have launched a formal investigation into a targeted act of extreme violence after a riot police officer was struck by an incendiary device during a weekend of civil unrest in Paris. The incident, now being treated with the highest level of legal scrutiny, marks a volatile escalation in the ongoing friction between the state and demonstrators.

Medical reports indicate the officer suffered significant injuries when a Molotov cocktail was deployed amidst the crowds. The French prosecutor's office confirmed that the probe is focusing on the intentional harm caused during the skirmish, as forensic teams begin the process of identifying individuals from various street-level surveillance feeds across the capital.

> "The thin line between civil disobedience and targeted hostility has blurred, challenging the very foundations of democratic protest in the heart of Europe."

The gravity of the situation has reignited a national debate regarding the safety of frontline responders and the limits of public assembly. With tensions remaining high across the city, the outcome of this investigation is expected to set a precedent for how French law handles extremist elements within broader social movements.
