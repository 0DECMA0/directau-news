Victoria Police have formally identified the 21-year-old woman found deceased in a Carlton residential complex as Jayda Allen Bonner. Emergency services were called to the Bouverie Street apartment block late yesterday evening, where the young woman was pronounced dead at the scene, sparking a major investigation into the circumstances surrounding her passing.

Family members of Ms Allen Bonner have expressed profound grief and frustration, revealing to the ABC that they had harboured significant fears for her safety prior to the incident. According to relatives, multiple attempts were made to seek external intervention and support for the young woman, raising serious questions regarding the efficacy of existing protective frameworks for those deemed at risk.

> "This tragic loss underscores a systemic failure to bridge the gap between reported concerns and proactive protection for the most vulnerable members of our community."

Homicide Squad detectives remain on-site as they work to determine the exact timeline of events leading up to the discovery. While the investigation is in its preliminary stages, authorities are treating the case as suspicious and are urging anyone with information or relevant dashcam footage from the Carlton area to contact Crime Stoppers immediately.
