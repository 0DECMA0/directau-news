After sixteen years at the forefront of Australia’s circular economy, the national Garage Sale Trail has announced its final season, marking the conclusion of a programme that saw over 7.2 million items find new homes. What began as a local grassroots initiative has transformed into a cornerstone of community sustainability, involving hundreds of local councils and millions of participants nationwide.

Organisers confirmed that the upcoming event will serve as the initiative's swan song, concluding a decade and a half of efforts to divert waste from landfills. The decision comes at a time when the second-hand market has reached unprecedented mainstream acceptance, largely due to the groundwork laid by this very movement since its Sydney beginnings in 2010.

> "The sunsetting of this national institution marks not an end to the mission, but the completion of a cultural shift where the second-hand economy has finally moved from the fringes to the heart of the Australian household."

As preparations begin for the final weekend of trade, the focus remains on the immense social and environmental dividend paid by the trail over the years. Australians are encouraged to register one last time, ensuring the event concludes with the same spirit of community and environmental stewardship that defined its sixteen-year run across the continent.
