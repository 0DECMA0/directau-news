A regional Victorian community is in mourning following a catastrophic multi-vehicle collision in Ledcourt that claimed the lives of three individuals, including a young couple dedicated to the state's healthcare sector. The incident, which occurred on the Western Highway, has sent shockwaves through the local medical fraternity where the pair were regarded as rising stars with promising futures in the Australian medical system.

Friends and colleagues have paid tribute to the couple, describing them as "loving" and "caring" professionals who had moved to the region to serve the community. Their loss is being felt acutely across Victoria’s regional health network, highlighting the profound human cost often associated with transit on our rural arterial roads and the vulnerability of those who serve the public.

> "This tragedy serves as a harrowing reminder of the fragile thread connecting our regional essential services to the safety of the infrastructure they traverse daily."

Major Collision Investigation Unit detectives remain on the scene to determine the precise circumstances surrounding the incident. As the investigation continues, the primary focus remains on supporting the grieving families and the healthcare teams who worked alongside the victims during their impactful, yet tragically brief, careers in the Grampians region.
