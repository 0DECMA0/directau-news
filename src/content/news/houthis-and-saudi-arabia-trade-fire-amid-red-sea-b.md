Houthi rebels have targeted the southern Saudi Arabian city of Jazan with a missile strike, intensifying an already volatile security situation in the region. The offensive follows a series of defensive strikes by Riyadh against military positions in Yemen, aimed at curbing the Iran-backed group's ongoing blockade of the Red Sea.

The escalating exchange of fire has raised alarms across international capitals, as the disruption of one of the world's most vital maritime corridors continues to impact global supply chains. Saudi authorities have reiterated their commitment to protecting national sovereignty, while the rebel forces show no signs of relenting in their strategic maritime campaign.

> "The convergence of regional territorial disputes and the weaponisation of global trade routes presents a grave challenge to international stability that transcends local borders."

As diplomatic efforts to de-escalate the situation remain stalled, the international community faces the growing prospect of a prolonged conflict. The economic consequences of the Red Sea blockade, coupled with the threat of expanded regional hostilities, remain a primary concern for major global powers and trade organisations alike.
