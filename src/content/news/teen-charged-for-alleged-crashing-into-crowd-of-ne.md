A teenager has been formally charged and refused bail following a critical incident in which a vehicle allegedly ploughed into a group of Newcastle Knights supporters. The New South Wales Police Force confirmed that the young driver was apprehended at the scene after the vehicle struck multiple pedestrians near the sports precinct following a recent match.

The Hunter community has been left deeply shaken by the event, which saw emergency responders treating several individuals for injuries on-site. Local authorities have spent the last 24 hours gathering witness statements and forensic evidence to establish the exact sequence of events that led to the vehicle entering the pedestrian-heavy zone during post-match celebrations.

> "This incident serves as a harrowing reminder of the fragile line between public celebration and unforeseen catastrophe, highlighting the urgent need for a rigorous review of safety protocols in our shared community spaces."

The accused remains in custody and is expected to face a magistrate in the coming days. Investigators are currently appealing to the public for any mobile phone or dashcam footage that may assist in finalising the brief of evidence as the legal proceedings move forward.
