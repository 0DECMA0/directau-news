A man has tragically lost his life this morning following a shark encounter at Sorrento Beach in Perth’s northern suburbs. Emergency services were called to the scene at approximately 10:30 am after witnesses reported a large predator had engaged a swimmer who was in the water with a companion at the time.

Western Australia Police and Surf Life Saving crews arrived shortly after to find the beach in a state of shock, with immediate closures implemented from Hillarys Boat Harbour to Trigg Beach. Authorities are currently working to support the victim's family and those who witnessed the incident, while Fisheries officers conduct a sweep of the coastal waters to identify the species involved.

> "This sobering event underscores the inherent risks of our coastal lifestyle and the profound, unpredictable power of the marine environment we share."

Local councils have advised residents to stay clear of the water for the next 48 hours as the investigation continues. While shark sightings are not uncommon in the region, the severity of this morning’s event has sent ripples through the tight-knit Perth swimming community, reigniting discussions regarding beach safety and mitigation strategies along the WA coastline.
