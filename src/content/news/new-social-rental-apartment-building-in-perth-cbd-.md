Construction has officially commenced on a major residential development in the heart of the Perth CBD, marking a significant milestone in the State Government’s efforts to address the city’s pressing housing shortage. The new apartment complex is designed to provide high-quality, long-term rental options for Western Australians who are increasingly priced out of the inner-city market.

The high-rise development will deliver 174 one- and two-bedroom apartments, strategically located to offer residents proximity to essential services and employment hubs. According to government specifications, 139 of these units are designated as affordable housing for low-to-moderate income earners, while the remaining 35 will be allocated for social housing to support the state’s most vulnerable citizens.

> "This project represents a structural shift in urban planning, acknowledging that a thriving city centre must remain accessible to the essential workers who sustain its daily operations."

By integrating social and affordable rentals into the CBD’s fabric, the initiative aims to reduce the burden on the existing waitlist and foster a more diverse urban community. The project is a key component of the current administration’s broader strategy to bolster housing stock and improve cost-of-living outcomes across Western Australia.
