A 47-year-old man has been taken into police custody following the discovery of a woman and two children deceased at a residence in Sydney's south-west. New South Wales Police established a crime scene at the Hinchinbrook property early this morning after what investigators are treating as a catastrophic domestic violence-related incident.

Emergency services were called to the suburban home following reports of a serious disturbance, where they encountered the distressing scene. Specialist forensic teams and homicide detectives are currently scouring the premises for evidence, while officers conduct door-to-door enquiries to piece together the final moments leading up to this tragedy.

> "This unspeakable tragedy serves as a harrowing reminder of the silent crisis of domestic insecurity that continues to haunt the fabric of our suburban communities."

Local authorities have urged anyone with information or CCTV footage from the surrounding area to contact Crime Stoppers immediately. As the community reels from the news, support services have been mobilised for first responders and those affected by the loss. If you or someone you know is impacted by family violence, please call 1800RESPECT or Lifeline on 13 11 14.
