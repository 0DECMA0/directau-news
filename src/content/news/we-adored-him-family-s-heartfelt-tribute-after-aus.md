A veteran Victoria Police sergeant has tragically lost his life following a significant fall while trekking the Inca Trail in Peru. The officer, who was off-duty at the time, was navigating a challenging mountain path toward the historic site of Machu Picchu when he plummeted approximately 400 metres from a narrow track.

His family has issued a poignant tribute, describing him as a deeply loved husband and father whose presence will be missed by all who knew him. Victoria Police confirmed the news with a heavy heart, noting the officer’s decades of dedicated service to the community. International authorities are currently working with Australian consular staff to facilitate the necessary arrangements for his return home.

> "This tragedy underscores the inherent risks that accompany even the most storied global pilgrimages, where the line between an adventurous spirit and environmental hazard remains perilously thin."

Colleagues across the force have expressed their profound shock, remembering a man of immense integrity and professional excellence. The incident has cast a shadow over the popular trekking route, prompting renewed discussions regarding safety protocols on high-altitude trails. As the investigation into the circumstances continues, the Australian community stands in solidarity with a family grieving an unimaginable loss.
