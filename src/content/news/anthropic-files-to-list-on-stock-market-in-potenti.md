Artificial intelligence powerhouse Anthropic has formally lodged paperwork for a Nasdaq listing, seeking a staggering valuation of $US1.5 trillion. This move, which could see the Claude developer go public as soon as next month, represents one of the most ambitious floats in the history of the technology sector, despite a backdrop of significant fiscal challenges.

The San Francisco-based firm, a primary rival to OpenAI, has pursued this massive public offering despite reporting a $US114 billion loss in the first half of 2026. This stark contrast between market valuation and current profitability highlights a growing trend among global investors to prioritise long-term dominance in the generative AI race over immediate balance sheet stability.

> "The sheer scale of this float suggests a market prioritising future dominance over current fiscal solvency, setting a high-stakes precedent for the AI era."

Local analysts suggest that while the capital injection will fuel Anthropic's research and development, the sheer volume of the burn rate remains a point of contention. As the listing approaches, the global financial community will be watching closely to see if the market has the appetite to support a trillion-dollar entity that is yet to find its path to profitability.
