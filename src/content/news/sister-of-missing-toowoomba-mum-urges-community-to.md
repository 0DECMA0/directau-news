Faith Isaacs, the sister of missing Toowoomba mother Jana Armstrong, has issued a poignant plea to the Darling Downs community, urging residents to sustain their search efforts as the investigation enters a critical phase. Speaking on behalf of the family, Ms. Isaacs expressed profound gratitude for the volunteer spirit that has defined the region’s response since Jana was first reported missing.

Authorities and local search parties have spent several days scouring the area, yet definitive leads remain scarce despite the intensive mobilization of resources. Ms. Isaacs emphasised that even the smallest piece of information could prove vital to the Queensland Police Service's ongoing efforts to establish a timeline of events and locate her sister.

> "In the unsettling silence of a disappearance, a community’s enduring vigilance acts as the only bridge between a painful mystery and the resolution every family deserves."

As the search perimeter continues to be monitored, the family has requested that locals remain observant during their daily routines and report any irregularities to Crime Stoppers immediately. The collective hope remains that a renewed focus from the public will provide the necessary breakthrough to bring Jana Armstrong safely home.
