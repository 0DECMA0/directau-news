The Northern Territory Department of Corrections has formally suspended three officers on full pay following a significant security breach that resulted in a high-stakes, 48-hour search for an inmate who fled custody on Sunday.

The incident triggered an intensive multi-agency operation across the region before the individual was successfully apprehended, prompting authorities to initiate a comprehensive internal review into the facility's operational protocols and oversight mechanisms.

> "The integrity of our justice system rests upon the absolute reliability of its custodial barriers, and any breach of this nature demands a rigorous interrogation of current safety standards."

While the investigation remains in its preliminary stages, the decision to stand down staff underscores the gravity of the lapse as the government faces mounting pressure to address systemic vulnerabilities within the Territory's correctional infrastructure.
