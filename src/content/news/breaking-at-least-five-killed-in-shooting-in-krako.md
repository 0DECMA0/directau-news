At least five people have been confirmed deceased and approximately twelve others injured following a major shooting incident in the heart of Krakow, Poland’s second-largest city. Local emergency services responded to reports of gunfire in a high-traffic area, immediately cordoning off the precinct as they scrambled to provide medical assistance to the victims.

The gravity of the situation has placed Polish security forces on high alert, as the nation grapples with one of its most significant violent events in recent memory. Eyewitnesses described a scene of chaos and confusion, while forensic teams have now established a perimeter to begin the arduous task of piecing together the timeline of the assault.
