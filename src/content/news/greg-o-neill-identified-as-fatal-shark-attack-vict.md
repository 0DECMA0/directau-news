Authorities have formally identified the 63-year-old man who tragically lost his life following a shark encounter on Friday as Greg O’Neill. The veteran waterman was a well-known figure within the coastal community, and his passing has triggered a profound wave of mourning across the region as friends and neighbours come to terms with the sudden loss.

Family members released a poignant statement on Saturday, remembering Mr O’Neill not merely as a victim of a maritime tragedy, but as a devoted family man whose soul was inextricably linked to the sea. Despite the swift and professional response from surf lifesavers and paramedics who arrived on the scene within minutes, the nature of the incident was such that he could not be saved.

> "This tragedy strikes at the heart of our coastal identity, reminding us that the ocean’s majesty carries an inherent and unpredictable risk that even the most experienced watermen respect."

In the aftermath of the event, local government officials have implemented standard safety protocols, including the temporary closure of nearby beaches. Fisheries officers are currently conducting patrols to monitor the area, while the broader community remains focused on providing support to the O’Neill family during this incredibly difficult period of grief and reflection.
