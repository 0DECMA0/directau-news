Anthony Marsh has successfully defended the seat of Nepean for the Liberal Party, securing a victory in a tightly contested by-election that saw local voters reaffirm their preference for the Coalition. The result follows a high-stakes campaign where the party managed to maintain its grip on the crucial electorate despite a robust challenge from independent candidates.

The final count confirmed that Marsh’s lead remained insurmountable as preferences were distributed, reflecting a steady performance in key booths across the district. Observers noted that the campaign’s focus on regional infrastructure and cost-of-living pressures resonated effectively with the local demographic, ensuring the seat did not slip from the party’s grasp.

> "This victory represents more than just a retained seat; it is a clear signal that the electorate is prioritising established representation as a buffer against broader economic instability."

With the result now formalised, the focus shifts to the legislative agenda as the Liberal Party looks to build on this momentum. For the leadership, the retention of Nepean provides a vital measure of stability and a clear mandate to continue their current policy trajectory within the state's political landscape.
