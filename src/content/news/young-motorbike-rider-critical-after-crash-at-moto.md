A 14-year-old boy is fighting for life in a Melbourne hospital following a high-speed collision during a junior motocross championship in Victoria’s west. Emergency services were dispatched to the regional track on Saturday afternoon after the teenager collided with another rider during a competitive heat, resulting in what authorities describe as life-threatening injuries.

First responders treated the youth at the scene before he was airlifted in a critical condition. The second rider involved in the incident was assessed by paramedics and is believed to have escaped serious physical harm, though the event was promptly suspended as officials and Victoria Police established a perimeter to investigate the circumstances of the crash.

> "The inherent risks of high-octane youth sports are once again under the spotlight as a community rallies behind a young life hanging in the balance."

Motorcycling Victoria and local club officials are expected to review safety protocols and track conditions as part of the standard inquiry into the incident. For now, the focus of the tight-knit regional sporting community remains firmly on the recovery of the young athlete, with support services being made available to witnesses and family members affected by the tragedy.
