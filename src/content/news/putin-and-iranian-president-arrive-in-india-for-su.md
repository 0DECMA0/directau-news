The arrival of these high-profile figures in New Delhi underscores a concerted effort by the BRICS nations to forge a cohesive alternative to Western-led international frameworks. As host, India finds itself at a strategic crossroads, tasked with navigating the complex interests of a diverse membership while the world watches how this collective addresses the gravest security challenges of the decade.
