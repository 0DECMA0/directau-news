Ukraine's capital has endured one of the most significant aerial bombardments since the commencement of hostilities, as Russian forces launched a massive ballistic missile strike on Kyiv overnight. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed the scale of the Sunday morning assault, describing it as one of the most intense barrages directed at the city to date.

Local defence systems were engaged across the metropolitan area for several hours, with residents seeking shelter as the sound of interceptions echoed throughout the district. This latest escalation marks a strategic shift in the intensity of the conflict, aimed squarely at the nation's administrative and civilian heart.

> "This surge in high-velocity ordnance marks a grim milestone in the endurance of urban centres, testing the absolute limits of modern air defence infrastructure."

International observers are closely monitoring the fallout from this weekend’s events, as the sheer volume of ballistic hardware deployed signals a potential new phase in the protracted struggle. For now, the city remains on high alert as emergency services manage the aftermath in the morning light.
