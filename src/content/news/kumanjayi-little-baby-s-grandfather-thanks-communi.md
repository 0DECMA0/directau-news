The family of five-year-old Kumanjayi Little Baby has expressed profound gratitude to the Alice Springs community following the discovery of the young boy’s remains on the town's outskirts last Thursday. In a formal statement released through family elders, the grieving relatives acknowledged the national outpouring of grief while firmly calling for calm as the legal system begins its formal proceedings.

The boy's grandfather, acting as a spokesperson for the family, urged the public to allow the Northern Territory Police and the judiciary the necessary space to investigate the circumstances surrounding this tragedy. Tributes have continued to mount at various sites across Central Australia, reflecting a community deeply shaken by a loss that has resonated far beyond the borders of the Territory.

> "In the quietude of profound loss, a community’s strength is measured not by its outcry, but by its unwavering commitment to the integrity of the path toward truth."

As the investigation remains active, Northern Territory authorities have maintained a heavy presence in the region to ensure public order and the preservation of evidence. The family has requested privacy in the coming weeks to conduct traditional ceremonies and to honour the memory of a young life taken far too soon, while the nation waits for the wheels of justice to turn.
