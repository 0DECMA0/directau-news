The House of Representatives observed an extraordinary pause in legislative proceedings today as members gathered to express solidarity with the Independent Member for Indi, Helen Haines. In a poignant display of support following the recent physical confrontation targeting the MP, an orange cardboard cockatoo—a symbol of her community and political identity—was placed in her vacated seat on the crossbench.

The suspension of ordinary business allowed representatives from all sides of the political divide to offer tributes and messages of recovery. The atmosphere in the Chamber remained unusually hushed, reflecting the gravity of the safety concerns now facing public officials. Prime Minister Anthony Albanese and Opposition Leader Peter Dutton joined crossbench colleagues in condemning the incident that led to Dr. Haines' absence.

> "This silent sentinel serves as a stark reminder that while political disagreements are the lifeblood of democracy, the safety and sanctity of our elected representatives must remain inviolable."

As investigations into the incident continue, the presence of the vibrant cutout served as a temporary placeholder for a voice currently silenced. The gesture underscores a growing collective concern within the halls of Parliament House regarding the escalation of hostility directed toward lawmakers in the current political climate.
