A third woman has officially engaged legal representation in relation to the escalating scandal involving the Sydney Swans, significantly heightening the legal and reputational stakes for the AFL club. Legal counsel confirmed on Monday that the complainant is currently assessing the viability of a civil lawsuit following what has been described as an 'incredibly traumatic' series of events that have left those involved seeking formal accountability.

The development marks a significant expansion of the case, which has already seen two other individuals come forward with serious allegations regarding the club's internal conduct. Legal experts suggest that the involvement of multiple parties now points toward a potential pattern of behaviour, which may require a comprehensive investigation into the club's historical culture and its protocols for handling sensitive grievances.

> "In the court of public opinion, the silence of a prestigious institution is often louder than the testimonies of those it failed to protect."

As the matter moves toward potential litigation, the Sydney Swans management faces mounting pressure to provide a transparent account of the incidents in question. The AFL has been briefed on these latest developments, though a formal statement regarding the third complainant’s specific legal strategy remains pending as the brief is further finalised.
