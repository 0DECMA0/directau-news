A collaborative masterwork documenting one of the nation’s most scrutinized legal sagas has secured the top honour at this year’s Australian Book Industry Awards. The publication, authored by literary heavyweights Helen Garner, Chloe Hooper, and Sarah Krasnostein, chronicles the complex proceedings of the Erin Patterson trial, marking a significant milestone in the country’s rich tradition of high-calibre reportage.

The project saw the three esteemed writers converge on the courtroom in May 2025, blending their distinct investigative styles to capture the atmospheric tension of the regional Victorian judiciary. The resulting volume has been lauded by the judging panel for its psychological depth and its refusal to lean into the sensationalism that often plagues the genre of narrative non-fiction.

> "The synthesis of three peerless literary voices has transformed a grim legal proceeding into an enduring examination of the Australian psyche and the fragility of truth."

This accolade reaffirms the enduring appetite for quality long-form journalism in the domestic market. Industry analysts suggest that the victory signals a shift towards collaborative authorship in prestige publishing, as the book continues to resonate with a public seeking clarity on a case that deeply affected the national consciousness.
