Seven people have been confirmed deceased and an intensive search and rescue operation is currently underway after a vessel capsized off the coast of northern Cyprus. Emergency services were alerted late yesterday when the craft began to founder in regional waters, sparking an immediate response from maritime authorities.

The vessel, which several local media outlets have identified as a catamaran, reportedly began taking on water approximately four miles from the shoreline. While initial rescue efforts managed to reach the site quickly, the vessel had already succumbed to the intake, leaving a significant number of passengers at the mercy of the sea.

> "This maritime disaster serves as a grim reminder of the inherent vulnerabilities faced by those navigating these busy but often treacherous Mediterranean corridors."

Investigations into the cause of the incident and the vessel's point of origin are ongoing as officials attempt to establish a manifest. While seven bodies have been recovered, the total number of those still unaccounted for remains unconfirmed as regional units continue to scour the area for survivors.
