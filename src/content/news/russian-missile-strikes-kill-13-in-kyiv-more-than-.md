At least 13 people have lost their lives and more than 30 others are injured following a wave of Russian ballistic missile strikes on the Ukrainian capital, Kyiv. Local authorities reported on Tuesday that the barrage targeted several districts, causing extensive damage to residential buildings and a significant disruption to the city's power grid.

Rescue operations are currently underway as emergency services navigate the wreckage of apartment blocks and commercial sectors. This latest escalation represents one of the most intense aerial assaults on the metropolis in recent months, further straining the region's air defence capabilities and civilian morale during a critical phase of the conflict.

> "This calculated strike against the heart of the capital serves as a stark reminder that the cost of this conflict is increasingly borne by civilian populations and the essential infrastructure that sustains them."

International leaders have swiftly condemned the strikes, noting the precision and scale of the weaponry deployed against non-military targets. As the city works to restore electricity and assist the wounded, the geopolitical pressure on global allies to bolster Ukraine’s defensive systems is expected to reach a new fever pitch at the upcoming security summits.
