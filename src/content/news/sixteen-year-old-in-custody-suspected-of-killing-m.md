A sixteen-year-old youth remains in police custody following a tragic incident in Perth's south-eastern suburbs that resulted in the death of a woman and left a twelve-year-old girl with life-altering injuries. Western Australia Police were summoned to the family residence late yesterday evening, where they discovered the victims in what investigators are currently treating as an alleged family violence homicide.

Homicide Squad detectives have established an extensive crime scene at the property as they work to reconstruct the events leading up to the tragedy. The twelve-year-old victim was transported to Perth Children’s Hospital under priority conditions, where she continues to receive emergency medical treatment for serious wounds. Neighbours in the typically quiet residential area have expressed profound shock, describing the family as reserved and noting no prior visible signs of domestic discord.

> "This devastating event serves as a somber reminder of the invisible fractures that can exist within the domestic sphere, necessitating a deeper societal reflection on the complexities of family mental health and intervention."

Charges are expected to be finalised later today as forensic examinations of the site continue. Support services have been mobilised for the first responders and community members affected by the scene. At this stage, authorities have not released the names of the involved parties, pending further notification of extended family members across the state.
