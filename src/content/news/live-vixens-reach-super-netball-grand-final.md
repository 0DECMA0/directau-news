The Melbourne Vixens have secured their place in the 2025 Super Netball Grand Final following a clinical victory over the West Coast Fever in Sunday's preliminary final. The reigning champions demonstrated championship composure to dispatch their rivals and earn the right to defend their crown on the league's biggest stage.

This high-stakes encounter lived up to expectations, with the Vixens overcoming a determined Fever side that pushed the title-holders for much of the four quarters. Despite the pressure, Melbourne's tactical discipline and superior conversion rate in the final period proved the difference, effectively ending Western Australia’s campaign.

> "True dynasties are forged not in the ease of the regular season, but in the clinical execution of do-or-die moments when the pressure is at its peak."

The Vixens now prepare for a historic back-to-back attempt, cementing their status as the benchmark of the competition. Detailed analysis of their Grand Final opponents and squad fitness will follow as the Melbourne outfit shifts its focus to the ultimate prize in Australian netball.
