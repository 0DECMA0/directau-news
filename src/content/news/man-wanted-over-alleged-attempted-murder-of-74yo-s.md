New South Wales Police have issued an urgent arrest warrant for a 45-year-old man following a grave incident involving a 74-year-old victim in Sydney’s west on Saturday. Detectives from the local area command have intensified their search as they investigate the circumstances surrounding the alleged life-threatening confrontation.

Emergency services were called to the scene over the weekend, where a senior citizen was found with critical injuries. While the investigation remains ongoing, authorities have formally identified a suspect they believe is central to their inquiries regarding the violent assault that has shocked the local community.

> "The increasing vulnerability of our seniors in suburban settings demands not just a swift police response, but a serious societal reckoning on the protection of the elderly."

Police have cautioned members of the public against approaching the wanted individual and have urged anyone with information to contact Crime Stoppers immediately. The victim remains in a serious condition as investigators continue to piece together the events of Saturday afternoon.
