The Northern Territory Government has disclosed that the scale of the Jacana Energy smart meter rollout failure is significantly more extensive than previously reported, with approximately 5,000 customers now confirmed to be affected. This revelation marks a substantial increase from earlier estimates, as the state-owned retailer struggles to rectify systemic issues that have left thousands of Territorians without accurate or timely electricity bills for months.

Internal data suggests that the "bungle," as described by critics, stems from technical integration hurdles between the new hardware and existing billing systems. For many households, this delay has created a mounting financial burden, as they face the prospect of receiving backdated invoices for months of energy consumption during a period of rising living costs.

> "The true cost of this administrative oversight isn't just measured in delayed revenue, but in the erosion of public trust in the Territory’s essential service infrastructure."

As the government moves to address the backlog, calls for greater transparency regarding the procurement and implementation phases of the rollout continue to grow. Jacana Energy has stated it is working to resolve the data discrepancies, yet for the 5,000 customers currently in limbo, a definitive timeline for a return to billing normalcy remains elusive.
