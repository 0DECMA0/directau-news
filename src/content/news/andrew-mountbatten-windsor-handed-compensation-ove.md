The move highlights the ongoing logistical complexities involved in reallocating royal residences amidst shifting public sentiment. As the former prince retreats further from the periphery of official duties, the resolution of these maintenance liabilities serves as a pragmatic, albeit controversial, conclusion to his residency within the Windsor estate.
