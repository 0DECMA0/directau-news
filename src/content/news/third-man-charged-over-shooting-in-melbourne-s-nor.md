Detectives have apprehended a third man in connection with a fatal shooting that occurred in Melbourne’s northern suburbs earlier this month. The suspect was intercepted by Victoria Police at Melbourne International Airport while attempting to board a flight out of the country, marking a significant escalation in the ongoing investigation into the incident.

The arrest follows the previous charging of two other individuals, as Homicide Squad detectives continue to piece together the events that led to the tragic loss of life. Authorities had been monitoring the 24-year-old suspect’s movements, leading to a coordinated tactical intervention at the international terminal before he could depart Australian soil.

> "The intersection of suburban crime and border security highlights a growing desperation among those attempting to evade the reach of Victorian law enforcement, yet it also proves the efficacy of modern surveillance in high-stakes investigations."

The man is expected to face Melbourne Magistrates' Court today to answer for his alleged involvement in the matter. Police officials have indicated that the investigation remains active, with forensic teams still reviewing evidence recovered from the scene to ensure all parties involved are held accountable under the full weight of the law.
