Prosecutors have characterised British entertainer Russell Brand as a "ruthless, calculated and manipulative sexual predator" during a powerful opening address at his rape trial in London. The prosecution’s case, detailed before a packed courtroom, alleges that the comedian used his significant fame and influence to target and exploit individuals over a period of several years.

The proceedings represent a significant legal milestone for the former high-profile media personality, who has transitioned in recent years from a mainstream star to an alternative cultural commentator. As the Crown outlined the gravity of the charges, the court was told of a series of alleged incidents that legal counsel argues demonstrate a systematic pattern of predatory behaviour rather than isolated occurrences.

> "This trial marks a definitive moment where the theatre of celebrity meets the unyielding scrutiny of the bench, reminding us that no public persona is immune to the weight of historic accountability."

Brand has consistently denied all allegations of non-consensual activity, maintaining that his past relationships were always entered into by mutual agreement. The trial, which has drawn global media attention, is expected to continue for several weeks as the jury hears testimony from several complainants and examines evidence from a decade of the entertainer's life.
