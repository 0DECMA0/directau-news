Opposition frontbencher Andrew Hastie has issued a searing ultimatum to One Nation, demanding the party either retract or take full responsibility for a promotional video labelling him a "traitor." The escalation follows a period of heightened friction between the Liberal stalwart and the minor party, with Mr Hastie further branding Pauline Hanson and Barnaby Joyce "lying cowards" in a significant departure from standard parliamentary decorum.

The dispute centres on a contentious video produced by One Nation that scrutinises Mr Hastie’s record, a move that has sparked outrage within Coalition ranks. The Shadow Minister for Defence, a former SAS captain, has taken particular umbrage at the questioning of his loyalty to the Commonwealth, asserting that such rhetoric undermines the integrity of the nation's political institutions and historical standards of debate.

> "In the modern political arena, the weaponisation of 'loyalty' risks hollowing out the very democratic foundations it purports to protect, turning policy debate into personal character assassination."

As the war of words intensifies, the fallout threatens to complicate preferences and alliances ahead of the next federal election. While One Nation remains defiant, the broader political landscape is watching closely to see if this stoush signals a permanent fracturing of relations between the traditional conservative base and the populist right in Canberra.
