The Norwegian Royal House has announced the passing of Princess Astrid, the elder sister of the late King Harald V, at the age of 94. Her death on Friday occurred just 48 hours after the nation gathered in Oslo to bid a final, sombre farewell to King Harald, plunging the Scandinavian constitutional monarchy into an unprecedented period of dual mourning.

Princess Astrid was widely recognised for her unwavering commitment to public service, having frequently acted as the nation’s first lady during the reign of her father, King Olav V, and supporting her brother’s tenure with quiet dignity. Her departure marks the conclusion of a generation that steered Norway through the post-war era and into the modern age, leaving a significant void in the country's cultural and symbolic leadership.

> "The simultaneous departure of two monumental pillars of the House of Glücksburg represents a seismic shift in the Norwegian national fabric, marking the definitive end of a historic era of duty."

Official statements from the Palace indicate that Princess Astrid passed away peacefully, surrounded by her family. As international leaders and heads of state extend their condolences to the Norwegian people, the dual loss serves as a poignant reminder of the stability provided by the monarchy during a century of profound global change.
