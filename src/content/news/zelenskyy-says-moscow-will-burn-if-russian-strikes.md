Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has issued a stern ultimatum to the Kremlin following Kyiv's most expansive aerial operation against the Russian capital to date. In a series of overnight strikes, scores of long-range drones breached Moscow's airspace, marking a pivotal escalation in the ongoing conflict and signalling a shift in Ukraine's strategic reach.

Local authorities in Moscow reported an unprecedented volume of unmanned aerial vehicles, though they claim many were neutralised by air defence systems. The operation appears to be a direct response to sustained pressure on Ukrainian civilian infrastructure, with Kyiv now demonstrating its capacity to project force far beyond the immediate front lines in the country’s east.

> "This strategic shift represents more than just a military strike; it is a calculated effort to alter the domestic perception of a conflict that has, until now, felt distant to many within the Russian heartland."

As the international community watches with increasing concern, the potential for further volatility remains high. Mr Zelenskyy’s recent rhetoric suggests that these incursions will persist and potentially intensify unless the barrage against Ukrainian power grids and urban centres is halted, placing the Kremlin under immense pressure to reconsider its current trajectory.
