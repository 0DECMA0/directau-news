Hundreds of dignitaries, sporting greats, and members of the public gathered at Perth Stadium today to pay their final respects to Dennis Cometti, the legendary broadcaster who served as the definitive voice of Australian rules football for over five decades. The state memorial service provided a sombre yet celebratory occasion to honour a man whose linguistic precision and distinctive dry wit became synonymous with the nation's sporting identity.

Cometti, whose career spanned from his early days in Western Australian radio to his status as a national treasure on television, was remembered not just for his professional brilliance but for his immense contribution to the Australian cultural landscape. Colleagues and family members shared intimate reflections on his life, painting a picture of a humble individual who managed to turn the simple act of play-by-play commentary into a masterclass of the English language.

> "The passing of a voice so deeply woven into our collective memory represents the conclusion of an era where sports broadcasting was elevated to a genuine craft."

As the ceremony concluded, the sentiment across the ground was clear: the airwaves will be notably quieter without his presence. His legacy remains firmly entrenched in the annals of Australian media, providing a benchmark for excellence that will inspire broadcasters for generations to come.
