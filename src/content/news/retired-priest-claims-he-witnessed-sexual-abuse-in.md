A retired priest has broken decades of silence to corroborate allegations of historic misconduct at Our Lady of Dolours Catholic Church in Chatswood, as several former students come forward with claims of mistreatment. The testimony follows formal allegations from three men who assert they were targeted by Father Paul Francis Cahill during the 1970s and 1980s, marking a significant development in the pursuit of institutional accountability.

Legal representatives for the claimants suggest that the retired priest’s decision to speak out provides crucial validation for those who have carried the weight of these experiences for nearly fifty years. The allegations centre on a period when Father Cahill held a position of significant authority within the Sydney parish, raising serious questions about the oversight and internal reporting structures of the era.

> "When the sanctuary of the pulpit is shielded by institutional silence, it is only the courage of those who break that silence that can initiate a long-overdue reckoning with the past."

As the investigation into the school and parish's history continues, the Catholic Archdiocese is facing renewed calls for transparency regarding its handling of past grievances. The survivors, now in their later years, are seeking acknowledgement of the trauma they endured, while authorities examine whether further individuals may have been impacted by the events in question.
