The owner of the Porepunkah property where a violent confrontation occurred involving Dezi Freeman has come forward with new testimony, claiming he held a direct telephone conversation with Freeman in the immediate aftermath of the shooting of two Victoria Police officers. This significant development adds a fresh layer to the chronological sequence of events surrounding the high-profile incident that has deeply affected the Ovens Valley community.

Local authorities are now scrutinising the details of this communication to determine its relevance to the ongoing investigation into the conduct of the individual involved. The property owner, who has provided a formal account to investigators, suggests the dialogue offered an immediate insight into the suspect's state of mind following the discharge of firearms against law enforcement. The incident, which resulted in grave injuries to two members of the force, remains a primary focus for regional crime units.

> "In the wake of such a volatile breakdown of public safety, the sequence of communication often proves as critical as the physical evidence left at the scene."

As the matter progresses through the legal system, these fresh allegations are expected to be thoroughly examined by counsel during upcoming proceedings. Community leaders in the Porepunkah region have reiterated calls for a comprehensive investigation to ensure a complete understanding of the circumstances that led to the confrontation. Victoria Police have not yet provided a public response regarding the potential impact of this testimony on their current brief.
