A catastrophic collision between a train and a school minibus at a level crossing in Belgium has claimed the lives of four individuals, including two children, in what local authorities are describing as a profound tragedy. The incident occurred when the vehicle, which was carrying nine occupants at the time, was struck by an oncoming passenger service, leading to an immediate and massive response from emergency services.

Preliminary reports suggest that the impact decimated the minibus, leaving five survivors who were rushed to nearby hospitals with varying degrees of injury. Investigators are currently on-site examining the functionality of the level crossing's safety barriers and signalling systems to determine exactly why the vehicle was on the tracks as the train approached.

> "This harrowing event serves as a stark reminder that despite modern advancements in rail safety, the intersection of road and track remains one of the most vulnerable points in our transport infrastructure."

As the region enters a period of mourning, the Belgian government has promised a transparent and exhaustive inquiry into the disaster. The focus remains on providing critical support to the families affected by this devastating loss, while transport advocates renew calls for stricter safety protocols at crossings across the continent.
