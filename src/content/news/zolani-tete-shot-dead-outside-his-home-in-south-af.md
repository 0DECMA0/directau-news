The international boxing community is in mourning following reports that former two-weight world champion Zolani Tete has lost his life in a violent incident outside his residence in South Africa's Eastern Cape province. Local authorities confirmed the passing of the 36-year-old athlete, known affectionately to fans as "Last Born," marking a sombre day for a nation that regarded him as one of its finest pugilistic exports.

Tete's career was defined by extraordinary achievements, including world titles in the super-flyweight and bantamweight divisions and the record for the fastest knockout in a world title fight. As law enforcement officials begin their investigation into the circumstances surrounding the tragedy, the focus remains on the immense legacy left by a man who rose from humble beginnings to dominate the global stage with technical brilliance and power.

> "The sudden departure of a champion who navigated the rigours of the ring only to meet such a tragic fate at his doorstep serves as a stark reminder of the fragile nature of peace in the sporting heartland."

Tributes have begun to flow from colleagues and rivals alike, acknowledging Tete not just for his prowess within the squared circle, but for his character outside of it. His passing leaves a significant void in South African sport, as the fraternity grapples with the loss of a veteran who still had much to offer the next generation of fighters.
