North Queensland co-captain Jason Taumalolo has produced a vintage performance to snap a 1,426-day scoring drought, steering the Cowboys to a spirited comeback victory over the Gold Coast Titans. The veteran forward’s first try in nearly four years proved to be the emotional catalyst for a side that had struggled for rhythm in the opening stages of the contest, ultimately securing a vital two points in their pursuit of a finals berth.

Taumalolo, whose role has evolved into that of a high-workload middle distributor in recent seasons, had not crossed the stripe since the 2020 season. His drought-breaking effort underscored a dominant second-half display by the Townsville-based outfit, who capitalised on a lapse in the Titans' defensive discipline to overturn an early deficit and silence the Gold Coast faithful.

> "In the high-stakes theatre of professional rugby league, the return of a veteran champion to the scoresheet does more than add four points; it restores the collective belief of an entire organisation."

The result leaves the Gold Coast Titans searching for answers after failing to maintain their early momentum, while the Cowboys move closer to cementing their position within the top eight. For coach Todd Payten, the sight of his marquee powerhouse finding the white line serves as a timely reminder of the veteran leadership required as the National Rugby League season enters its critical final phase.
