The Canberra Raiders have etched their names into the history books, securing a clinical 6-0 victory over the Wests Tigers to progress in the NRLW finals series. In a contest defined by defensive resilience rather than offensive flair, the Green Machine capitalised on a solitary scoring opportunity to extinguish the Tigers' remarkable postseason ambitions and claim their first-ever finals win.

The match served as a masterclass in attrition, with both sides trading blows in a punishing encounter that tested the mettle of every player on the park. Despite the Tigers' spirited 'fairytale' run through the latter half of the season, they were unable to breach a Canberra line that remained impenetrable under the immense pressure of sudden-death football.

> "In the modern era of the women's game, we often celebrate the spectacular try, but this victory was forged in the unglamorous, tireless grit of a defensive wall that simply refused to buckle."

With this historic milestone achieved, the Raiders now shift their focus toward the next stage of the premiership hunt with significant momentum. For the Wests Tigers, while the disappointment of a finals shutout is palpable, the foundation laid during this campaign suggests the club has firmly established itself as a rising powerhouse in the national competition.
