The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT) has confirmed it is providing consular assistance to the man in custody, whose identity remains withheld pending formal charges under Thai law. Sources indicate that investigators are currently scrutinising CCTV footage and local travel records to establish a precise timeline of events leading up to the distressing find.
