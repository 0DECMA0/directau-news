The Australian Broadcasting Corporation’s head of investigative journalism, Jo Puccini, has announced her departure from the national broadcaster as a high-stakes internal review continues to unfold. This senior leadership exit coincides with an intensifying probe into the professional conduct and editorial output of former Four Corners reporter Mahmood Fazal, whose work has recently come under significant scrutiny.

As the executive with direct oversight of the ABC’s flagship current affairs program, Puccini’s resignation marks a pivotal moment for the organization’s investigative arm. The ongoing inquiry into Fazal’s reporting has prompted a wider discussion regarding the rigour of editorial checks and the maintenance of the public broadcaster’s exacting standards at a time of heightened public accountability.

> "The departure of a senior editorial leader during a period of intense internal scrutiny suggests a broader reckoning with the systemic checks and balances that underpin our national broadcaster’s credibility."

The ABC has yet to name a permanent successor for the role as it navigates the fallout from the Fazal investigation. This leadership vacuum at the top of the investigative unit underscores the immense pressure on the institution to ensure absolute probity in its journalism while managing internal stability during a period of transition.
