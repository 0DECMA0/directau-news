President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has held high-stakes discussions with envoys representing the incoming Trump administration in Kyiv, as diplomatic ties between the two nations reach a critical juncture. The meeting arrives amidst heightened speculation regarding the future of American military aid and the resilience of the transatlantic alliance under new leadership.

Florida Senator Marco Rubio, slated to lead the State Department, has sought to steady the waters by denying reports that intelligence-sharing protocols have been restricted. While tensions remain palpable, Rubio maintained that the strategic flow of information continues, even as the world watches for a definitive shift in Washington's foreign policy priorities.
