Victoria Police have charged five males in connection with a series of targeted homophobic attacks orchestrated through dating applications across Melbourne. The arrests follow a dedicated investigation into a pattern of violent robberies and incidents where victims were allegedly lured to secluded locations under the guise of meeting for a social encounter.

The recent operation brings the total number of individuals accused of involvement in similar targeted incidents across the state to nearly 50. Authorities have noted a concerning trend where offenders exploit the anonymity of digital platforms to identify and isolate vulnerable members of the community, prompting a coordinated response from specialist crime squads.

> "This surge in targeted hostility via digital platforms reflects a precarious intersection between technological convenience and predatory intent, challenging the very fabric of our urban safety."

Detectives continue to urge the public to exercise extreme caution when arranging to meet individuals from dating apps, strongly recommending high-traffic public venues for all initial encounters. Investigators have signalled that further arrests may be forthcoming as they continue to examine potential links between various criminal syndicates operating across the metropolitan area.
