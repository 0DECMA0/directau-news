A premier private college in Canberra has launched an urgent internal investigation following reports that male students compiled and circulated a digital 'ranking' of their female peers based on physical appearance. The incident, which has sparked significant concern among the school community, surfaced after the disparaging content was shared across several social media platforms earlier this week.

School leadership confirmed that disciplinary measures are being considered as they work to identify the individuals responsible for creating and distributing the list. This development follows a series of similar incidents across Australian independent schools, highlighting ongoing challenges regarding digital culture and the maintenance of respectful relationships within high-pressure academic environments.

> "This trend reflects a broader cultural deficit where digital spaces are being weaponised against the dignity of young women, demanding more than just school-level discipline."

Support services have been made available to the students targeted by the list, and the institution has reinforced its commitment to fostering a safe and inclusive learning environment. Education authorities in the Australian Capital Territory are expected to monitor the situation closely as the internal probe concludes.
