Hundreds of mourners gathered in Victoria’s west on Friday to pay their final respects to 13-year-old Layla Jeffrey, a teenager remembered as a 'bright spark' whose life was tragically cut short. The service, held in her local community, saw a sea of people wearing vibrant colours to celebrate a young life that touched many before her remains were discovered earlier this month following an intensive search.

The emotional ceremony focused on Layla’s spirited nature and her deep connection to her family, providing a somber contrast to the gravity of the ongoing criminal investigation. A teenage boy has been charged in connection with her death and remains under the jurisdiction of the children’s court as the legal process moves forward, leaving a small town to grapple with an unimaginable loss.

> "The shattering of a young life in our regional heartland is not merely a private tragedy, but a profound tremor that shakes the very foundation of our social fabric and our collective responsibility toward the vulnerable."

As the community begins the arduous process of healing, local support services have been bolstered to assist those affected by the tragedy. The legal proceedings continue to develop, with the matter expected to return to court for a committal mention later this year, while the memory of a 'bright spark' remains etched in the hearts of the Victorian public.
