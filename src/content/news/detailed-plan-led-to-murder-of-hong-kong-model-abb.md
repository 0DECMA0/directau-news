The ex-husband and former in-laws of Hong Kong socialite Abby Choi have formally pleaded not guilty to charges relating to her death, as the High Court heard allegations of a meticulously orchestrated plan. The proceedings, which have captured global attention, follow the discovery of the influencer’s remains at a Tai Po village house in early 2023, an event that sent shockwaves through the financial hub’s social circles.

Prosecutors outlined a case suggesting the incident was not a spontaneous tragedy but the result of significant prior coordination. Alex Kwong, alongside his father and brother, denied the charges of taking the 28-year-old’s life, while his mother pleaded not guilty to perverting the course of justice. The court was briefed on the extensive forensic investigation that led authorities to a suburban property outfitted with equipment allegedly intended for the disposal of evidence.

> "This case transcends the typical bounds of criminal proceedings, highlighting a chilling intersection between familial ties and a calculated betrayal that has left the international community searching for answers."

As the trial progresses, the focus remains on the complex financial and personal motivations alleged by the Crown. Legal experts suggest the complexity of the forensic evidence and the high-profile nature of the victim will ensure this remains one of the most significant legal battles in the region’s recent history. The defendants remain in custody as the judicial process continues to unfold.
